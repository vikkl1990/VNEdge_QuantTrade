AI-Powered Crypto Trading Engine

VN Edge

VN Edge is a fully automated cryptocurrency trading engine that combines multi-scanner signal detection with machine learning ranked candidates and regime-aware execution. It continuously monitors the market across multiple timeframes and pairs, identifying high-probability setups in real time.

Built on a three-engine architecture, VN Edge uses LightGBM and gradient boosted models to rank and filter trade candidates. Regime detection ensures the system adapts to trending, ranging, quiet, and volatile conditions, while multi-layered risk management protects capital through VWAP vetoes, HTF alignment checks, circuit breakers, and dynamic position sizing.

Whether in paper learning mode or live execution, VN Edge provides full transparency through this dashboard, real-time Telegram alerts, and detailed analytics on every trade taken.

🔍

7 Scanner Engine

Structure bounce, BOS/CHoCH, liquidity sweep, momentum breakout, mean reversion, order block, and volume profile scanners working in parallel across all monitored pairs.

🧠

ML Ranking

LightGBM and GBR hybrid models score every candidate signal. Pure candle math features, MFE-based soft labels, and 0.60+ AUC validation ensure only the highest quality setups pass through.

🌊

Regime-Aware

Real-time detection of trending, ranging, quiet, and volatile market conditions. The engine dynamically adjusts thresholds, position sizing, and scanner weights based on the current regime.

🛡

Risk Layers

VWAP hard veto, higher timeframe alignment, circuit breakers, maximum drawdown limits, correlation guards, and adaptive position sizing protect capital at every level.
